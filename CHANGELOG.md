# Changelog

Notable changes. The package is installed from GitHub, so a version here is the `version` in `package.json`, not an npm release.

## Unreleased

- MFD: WPT - USER WPT INFORMATION and NRST - NEAREST USER WPTS (Pilot's Guide; FG1000 has neither, drawn in the AUX pages' box style). User waypoints are `points` of type `WPT`. The WPT page spells an identifier (ENT on a new name creates one at the aircraft's position), steps its position by degrees and minutes, shows a reference to the nearest VOR and airport, lists every user waypoint and maps it; its PAGE MENU creates, renames, deletes (one or all) and moves to the present position (Waypoint Setup grey). Deleting one the flight plan uses removes it from the plan. Changes go out through `onChange` as `points` (and `route`). The NRST page lists up to 25 within 200 nm; ENT opens the WPT page, DIRECT TO takes one. Pan mode's ENT opens a user waypoint's page, and WAYPOINT ENTRY and DIRECT TO have the USER list. New module `user-wpt.ts`.
- Flight plan PAGE MENU: Hold At Waypoint (MFD, on the waypoint under the cursor) and Hold At Present Position (both units) open hold windows: inbound course, turns, legs by time or distance, LOAD?. A hold is a `route` entry after its fix carrying `hold: { course, turn, time?, distance? }` (`Hold`); the flight plan shows a HOLD row, the map a racetrack. Present position adds a PPOS user waypoint to `points` and goes direct to it. Remove Hold replaces Hold At Waypoint on a hold. New `sequenceLeg(route, activeLeg)` for hosts that sequence the plan: a hold stays active. CLR on a fix deletes its hold; inverting the plan drops holds.
- PFD and MFD: approach suspend and missed approach sequencing (Pilot's Guide). Past the loaded approach's missed approach point the units set new state `susp` (reported through `onChange`): the MAP stays active, the PFD's OBS softkey reads SUSP, lit, and the HSI's GPS annunciation reads SUSP. SUSP, or ACTIVATE MISSED APPROACH, makes the leg from the MAP to the first missed approach fix active. No SUSP in OBS mode. A direct-to, loading an approach or leaving the MAP clears it. `pastMap`, `suspended` and `missedPatch` in `nav.ts`; `Glass.vue` shares `susp`.
- Pan mode (Pilot's Guide): the pointer flashes; 60 s without joystick input ends pan mode and recenters the map (MFD and PFD inset); on the MFD, ENT with the pointer on an airport, VOR, NDB or intersection opens its WPT page, and the joystick push or CLR goes back to the map; the flight plan page's map pans too. `pan.ts` gains `panUnder`, `PanIdle`, `PAN_IDLE_MS` and `PAN_WPT_PAGES`.
- Fixed: on the bezel's RANGE knob, the hover glow sat beside the printed arrows: FG1000's boxes for the knob and the up and down arrows are 6 px right of the art, and those for left and right 8 px low. The knob, its arrows, - and + and the diagonals now sit on the art. Text on the displays and the DISPLAY BACKUP button no longer selects on a click or drag.
- MFD: PROC's SELECT APPROACH opens a full PROC - APPROACH LOADING page, as the Pilot's Guide has it (was a window over the page): the approach previewed on the map, the airport, approach, transition, minimums and sequence boxes, LOAD? and ACTIVATE?. The PROCEDURES menu stays a window over the page. Both units' PROC flows gain BARO minimums between the transition and LOAD? (ENT skips them), opening on the minimums already set; loading sets `minimums` (null for OFF), now in `MfdState` and reported through `onChange`. The minimum altitude stops at 16,000 ft here and on TMR/REF. `ProcFlow` gains `baro` and `loading()` (with the approach's `points` for the map). The demo and `Glass.vue` share `minimums` between the units.
- Fixed: the altimeter's and airspeed tape's numbers, the VSI pointer's, the pitch ladder's and the map labels stepped a whole pixel at a time while their ticks glided, so a slow climb looked jittery in Chrome. They now move as smoothly as the ticks. The demo's autopilot flies every frame instead of ten times a second.
- Fixed: with two displays on a page and one hidden with `display:none`, the other's bezel keys drew black. Both displays inline the same FlightGear art, so its gradient and filter ids repeated and every `url(#id)` used the hidden copy. `buildDisplay` now prefixes each display's referenced ids (`scopeIds` in `display.ts`); ids nothing references keep their names.
- Font: the screens now draw all text in Saira Semi Condensed Medium, bundled under the SIL Open Font License (`src/fonts/`), instead of naming Liberation Sans Narrow and falling back to whatever the machine has. Digits are tabular. The displays load the font file through `new URL(..., import.meta.url)`, which bundlers such as Vite and webpack 5 emit as an asset, so a site's CSP can keep `font-src 'self'`. Text widths change with it.
- MFD: the windows follow the trainer's: a light grey frame, black boxes under stepped titles. PAGE MENU and the flight plan tools' windows are dark grey and sized to their contents, with a full-width cursor bar; MAP SETUP, DIRECT TO, the waypoint approach panel (and PROC - APPROACH LOADING's) and the AUX pages are full-height and mid grey; the PROCEDURES menu is mid grey and fits its options. `Highlighter.set` takes an optional `span` for a row-wide cursor.
- Fixed: a PAGE MENU option the cursor had passed over turned white when the list scrolled. `Highlighter.set(t, false)` now clears only its own black, not a color the page set.
- Autopilot: the GFC 700 flight director and autopilot (Pilot's Guide). `Afcs` (in `afcs.ts`, exported from the package) takes the AFCS keys and the yoke's AP DISC, CWS and GA, and each `update` returns the command bars (`fd`), whether the autopilot flies (`ap`) and the annunciations (`display`). New PFD state `afcs` draws the AFCS status box and FLC's airspeed reference or MAXSPD above the tape. New display option `afcsKeys` draws AP, FD, HDG, ALT, NAV, VNV, APR, BC, VS, FLC, NOSE UP and NOSE DN in the bezel's left column (on by default for the MFD); new bezel inputs of those names and `AFCS_SPOTS` in `bezel.ts`. The demo wires it and flies its sliders toward the command bars, with AP DISC, CWS and GA buttons and a glideslope slider. `Glass.vue` takes `afcs`, `fd` and `afcsKeys` props and emits `key` for an AFCS key.
- Keyboard typing: a letter or digit on the display last clicked types into the identifier under the cursor, Backspace clears back, Enter is ENT and Escape is CLR. New bezel input `CHAR` (character code) and `keyInput` in `bezel.ts`.
- Display failures (Line Maintenance Manual): `displayModes` in `display-backup.ts` sends the display left into reversionary mode, and the PFD's `failed` takes `com1`, `nav1`, `com2` and `nav2` (FG1000's red X over the radio field). The demo has fail PFD and fail MFD. `Glass.vue` takes a `reversionary` prop. `DisplayBackup`, `displayModes` and `RadioFailure` are exported from the package.
- PFD: reversionary mode (Pilot's Guide), new state `reversionary` and `engine`: the engine strip down the left, OAT under TAS, the inset map in the lower right, and ENGINE (LEAN, SYSTEM) on the first softkey. The demo's DISPLAY BACKUP button puts both units in it, a second PFD over the MFD. The engine strip's drawing and softkeys moved to `eis.ts`, shared with the MFD.
- MFD: AUX - TRIP PLANNING (AUTO or MANUAL, REM or a leg: trip, fuel, sunrise and sunset, density altitude and true airspeed), AUX - GPS STATUS (position, time, altitude, groundspeed and track; accuracy, RAIM and SBAS grey) and AUX - SYSTEM STATUS (LRUs in; airframe and database grey), from the Pilot's Guide. The sums are in `trip-planning.ts`.
- MFD: AUX - UTILITY's scheduler: ten One-time, Periodic or Event messages typed with the FMS knobs (`scheduler.ts`). New MFD option `onScheduler(message)` hands one to the host when it comes due; the demo adds it to the PFD's `alerts`.
- MFD: MAP SETUP's Airways group (AIRWAYS and the low and high airway ranges) and Profile group (grey). `shownAirways` takes the ranges; `AIRWAY_RANGE` exported from `map.ts`; `map-setup.ts` holds the groups.
- MFD: NRST - NEAREST AIRPORTS draws a dashed white line from the aircraft to the selected airport (Pilot's Guide). `MapData` gains `nearestLine`.
- PFD: the pitch ladder no longer moves its cut-off with the horizon. It shows inside a circle under the roll scale, wide enough for the 20° labels, cut flat at the heading box, and gains red extreme pitch chevrons (from 50° up and 30° down). The heading box sits on the turn rate band, whose ticks and trend vector now lie inside it; past 4°/s the trend vector ends in an arrowhead. New export `turnVector`. The airspeed tape gains its color strip, from new option `speedRanges` (default `SPEEDS_172S`), whose `vne` also reds the IAS box when `vne` is not set. The demo's pitch runs to ±80° and its turn rate follows bank and airspeed. Past +30°/-20° pitch or 65° bank the PFD declutters as the Pilot's Guide describes unusual attitudes, and their softkeys dim; new export `unusualAttitude`.

- Navdata: `buildNasr` builds V, T, J and Q airways from NASR's `AWY_SEG_ALT.csv` (optional in `NasrCsv`, any row order; `Nasr` gains `airways`, each point an id and its type), and `near` returns `airways` for the MFD, the segments within range with every point they use in `points`.
- MFD: the map's AIRWAYS key draws airways from new state `airways` (low grey, high green, with their range limits), and PROFILE or the PAGE MENU shows Profile View: altitude and distance scales, the aircraft, and its path on the map, with no terrain (Pilot's Guide). `shownAirways`, `profileView` and `symbolPoints` (which points the map draws: airway fixes past their range but still decluttered, a previewed fix once) in `map.ts`; `MapData` gains `airways` and `profilePath`.
- MFD: AUX - SYSTEM SETUP (time format and offset, MFD data bar fields, page navigation, nearest airport criteria) and AUX - UTILITY (timers, trip statistics, PAGE MENU resets), from the Pilot's Guide. The AUX group gains SYSTEM SETUP after GPS STATUS. `aux-pages.ts` holds their logic; `ete` moved there and is still exported from `glass-mfd`.
- MFD: WPT - APPROACH INFORMATION from the airport page's APR softkey: approaches, transitions, BARO minimums and the fix sequence, previewed on the map (Pilot's Guide). The airport page gains INFO-1, APR and grey DP, STAR and WX. `approachPoints` in `nav.ts`, the page's logic in `apr-info.ts`; `MapData` gains `preview`.
- MFD: Nearest Airports gains LD APR and a PAGE MENU (Select Airport/Runway/Frequency/Approach Window, Load Approach); ENT on the airport list moves to the next airport (Pilot's Guide). `ProcFlow.loadApproach`.
- PFD and MFD: the flight plan PAGE MENU's Closest Point Of FPL and Parallel Track, and on the MFD Create ATK Offset Waypoint, open their windows (Pilot's Guide). `MapPoint` gains `parallel`; `closestPointOfFpl`, `atkOffset`, `parallelTrack` and `cancelParallel` in `nav.ts`, the windows' logic in `fpl-tools.ts`. Direct-to, loading an approach and editing the plan cancel Parallel Track. The demo and the Vue component share `points` between the units, so a user waypoint reaches both.
- MFD: MAP SETUP from the navigation map's PAGE MENU: ORIENTATION and TRACK VECTOR (Pilot's Guide). `orientation` gains TRK UP and DTK UP. The demo's heading-up checkbox is gone; MAP SETUP sets it. Changed: heading, track and desired track are taken as magnetic, so HDG UP, TRK UP, DTK UP and the track vector add `magVar` to point true.
- MFD: the navigation map's Measure Bearing/Distance (Pilot's Guide), built on pan mode.
- Demo: a round red DISPLAY BACKUP button between the units (`display-backup.ts`); the displays have no reversionary mode yet.
- PFD and MFD: MENU in DIRECT TO offers Cancel Direct-To NAV, which resumes the flight plan on the closest leg (Pilot's Guide). PFD state gains `vtf`, which dims it during vector-to-final.
- Softkeys follow the G1000 trainer (v13.02, 172S): its layouts on the PFD and MFD menus, and dim keys for what is unavailable.
- PFD: the ALERTS key reads ADVISORY while a message is unread.
- PFD: the CDI label reads VOR1, LOC1, VOR2 or LOC2, and the course box DTK on GPS. `cdiLabel` and `isLocalizer` in `glass-cockpit-web/radio`.
- Changed: the PFD's speed references start off, as the trainer has them. A host that relied on the bugs showing by default sets `vspeedsOn`. TMR/REF follows the trainer's REFERENCES layout.
- PFD: `failed` shows FG1000's failure flags for attitude, airspeed, altitude, vertical speed and heading.
- PFD: the DME window and DME TUNING. `dmeText` in `glass-cockpit-web/radio`.
- MFD: PROC opens a PROCEDURES menu (activate vector-to-final, approach, missed approach), asks NOT APPROVED FOR GPS before activating a non-GPS approach, and activates vector-to-final with the VECTORS transition. New MFD state `vtf`, and route points PROC loads carry `approach`; `gpsApproved` and `vtfLeg` exported.
- Bezel: clicking an arrow at a knob's top corner turns it one detent (Shift: the outer ring), as clicking beside a knob does on the trainer; RANGE turns on its printed - and +. The wheel still works.
- Bezel: two pairs of arrows on dual knobs (outer and inner), arrows on HDG and the volume knobs, the joystick in eight directions; holding an arrow keeps turning. A fast wheel spin turns HDG and CRS 5°. The ALT knob pushes. Arrows glow under the pointer.
- PFD: the VSI scale stops at ±2000 fpm, its readout rounded to 50 fpm. MENU opens the PFD SETUP MENU, or the flight plan's PAGE MENU.
- MFD: MENU opens a PAGE MENU on the navigation map and flight plan pages (Declutter; Invert, Delete, Remove Approach). FPL again goes back to the previous page; PROC again closes PROCEDURES.
- Bezel: the left column is no longer shaded darker than the right.
- PFD: the pitch ladder no longer overlaps the roll pointer, and the turn rate indicator has half-standard and standard rate ticks.
- MFD and PFD inset: the joystick push starts pan mode: a pointer, and an info bar with the point under it, its distance and bearing, latitude and longitude. RANGE recenters on the pointer. DIRECT TO takes the point under the pointer.
- PFD: DIRECT TO and PROC open their windows, sharing the MFD's flow. New PFD state `procedures`, and `directFrom` so the inset draws a direct leg without a host round trip. Vue passes both from `mfd`.
- Bezel: holding COM ⇄ or CLR for 2 s is a long press, as Shift+click is.
- Demo: the PFD and MFD share their knobs and flight plan, so HDG, ALT, CRS, BARO and the radios on either unit change both. Vue: `mfd.selectedAlt`, when given, is shared by both units.
- Colors: the cursor is full cyan, and the page group window shows the selected page in cyan, the rest white, as on the trainer.
- ALT knob: the selected altitude stays on 100 ft steps, even when the host set an odd value. The demo slider steps 100 ft.
- `glass-cockpit-web/navdata`: NASR and CIFP transformations (`buildNasr`, `parseCifp`, `near`, `approachName`) moved from instrumentready.com, unchanged output. A separate export; the displays don't load it.
- Community docs: CONTRIBUTING, CODE_OF_CONDUCT, SECURITY, NOTICE, issue and PR templates. README reorganised.
- GitHub Actions CI: type check, unit tests, stale `dist/` check, install from git.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04)

First version, ported from FlightGear's FG1000.

- PFD: attitude, tapes, vertical speed, HSI with CDI, glideslope, bearing pointers, marker beacons, radios, active leg, flight plan window, and the softkey menus (inset map, nearest airports, transponder, wind, timer and references, alerts).
- MFD: engine strip, navigation and traffic maps, NRST, WPT, FPL with waypoint entry, checklists, DIRECT TO and PROC.
- Bezel: knobs, buttons and softkeys worked with the mouse, NAV/COM tuning and volume, CRS push.
- Vue components (`Glass.vue`, `Steam.vue`, `KitDemo.vue`) and `tuneNav` exported for instrumentready.com.
